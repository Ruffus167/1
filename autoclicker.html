Auto Clicker

Set a click interval and count, then use the floating control from any Hoilday page.

  1. Adjust the interval, click count, and start/stop hotkey in the floating controls.
  2. Move the pointer over a target in Hoilday and press Space (or your chosen key), or use Start in the floating panel.
  3. Press the hotkey again or choose Stop to stop. Runs are limited to 100,000 clicks and stop when you leave the page.

The clicker only runs on Hoilday pages. It simulates clicks on the element currently under your pointer; it does not click other sites or apps.
