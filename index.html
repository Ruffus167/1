    ✳ SHARE YOUR COLLECTION

    Gifts & trades.

    Gift an unlocked collectible or trade one for something you want.

    Send a gift

    Gifts move an item from your collection to another member. You cannot gift your last rank.

    Offer a trade

    Choose items from each collection. Members can accept, decline, or cancel offers. Bots rarely accept, and only when your offer is more valuable.

    Your trade offers

    Accepting a trade exchanges both items at once. If either item is no longer available, nothing will move.

    Sign in to see trade offers.

    YOUR ACCOUNT, YOUR BACKUP

    Export / import.

    Download your account progress, create a separate account, or restore a backup to the account you are signed into.

    Download account

    Save your username, profile picture, fish count, equipped rank, unlocked ranks and emojis, and completed side quests in a portable JSON backup. Your password is never included.

    Import or restore account

    A backup creates a separate account if its username is available. To restore a backup for the account you are signed into, use the same username; its current password is kept. Other existing accounts cannot be overwritten.
